## Quality through shorter learning and delivery cycles

[How Notion's Design Team Makes Great Products Quickly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M), Randy J. Hunt, Hatch Conference, 00:00 to 30:19.

| Source passage | Speaker's example or argument | Reusable adaptation in this skill |
|---|---|---|
| [00:31 to 03:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=31s), [14:14 to 16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=854s) | Hunt argues for quality in fast work. His account of craft combines judgment, practical technique, and repeated practice; quicker cycles can support improvement. He explicitly distinguishes this from rushing a release. | Select a small cycle that produces useful evidence or a checked improvement. Measure progress by what was learned or improved, without treating raw output or speed as sufficient evidence of quality. |
| [10:25 to 13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=625s), [16:08 to 17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=968s) | He argues that people close to building learn from the work, and that empowerment needs ownership of the outcome. He discusses smaller, flatter teams as his preferred organizational approach. | Use direct inspection, making, and feedback when they can resolve the decision. Retain responsibility for verification and follow-up. This skill neither prescribes a company reorganization nor converts a culture argument into authorization for external actions. |
| [17:43 to 20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=1063s) | The talk describes rapidly addressing product annoyances, then applies the same momentum to new features while keeping validation and communicating changes. | Distinguish a clear small fix from an uncertain new direction. Act within the existing scope, check the outcome, and record status so the next person knows what actually changed. A new research phase is not required for every fix. |
| [20:53 to 22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=1253s), [22:07 to 23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=1327s) | One feature used a thin approach that was easy to roll back and avoided a new data model. Keep independent work separable. A fast implementation still needs engineering review and the project's required checks; the anecdote supplies no universal line-count limit. |
| [24:13 to 26:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=1453s) | Hunt describes Notion's own arrangement: tools and skills stored in Notion, code as their design-system authority, research context, a playground sharing context and libraries, plus continued use of an IDE and canvas. | Reuse the current project's approved sources and components in a learning environment when practical. | [27:10 to 30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8odhmX32w2M&t=1630s) | The conclusion distinguishes moving toward a known goal from learning when the direction is uncertain. Define the observation needed for a learning test; use appropriate production checks for a committed change. Faster learning is available without pretending that a prototype is ready to release. |

The learning/delivery labels and compact decision record are Karim's implementation choices. The skill uses one review workflow because both talks inform the same decision: what useful, appropriately checked action to take next in this product's context.
