# Coordinated identity production

Use when logo, typography or palette needs practical development after understanding direction. Set each to new, refresh, keep or none. Fixed parts stay fixed; surface clashes explicitly.

Define one visual mechanism linking requested parts to audience, content and context. If exploration is needed, compare coherent identity cards using identical real content/applications, with strategic or structural differences instead of hue swaps. Recommend with reasons. Record approval only when given; do not force options after direction is chosen.

- Logo: intended sizes, monochrome/reverse, realistic backgrounds. Inspect 16px/32px use when relevant. Custom lettering/monograms are choices. Distinguish practical visibility from formal text contrast rules; logos have different accessibility treatment.
- Typography: source/license, weights, fallback, real numbers/punctuation and language coverage. For Arabic or other shaping scripts verify actual rendered text, shaping and directionality in the intended renderer.
- Palette: functional roles and actual foreground/background pairs per mode. Body text at least 4.5:1; large text/applicable non-text UI at least 3:1. Never round failure into a pass. Separate measured contrast from judgment.

Render representative applications at actual sizes. Colors/fonts do not prove trust or conversion. Deliver requested editable marks, type/palette roles, tokens, usage/misuse examples and font/license notes, proportional to scope. Existing design-dna remains the approved identity source.
