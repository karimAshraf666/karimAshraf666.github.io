# Awwwards reference research

Use Awwwards as another source for requested website direction, motion patterns or visual mechanisms. Start with the brief and dominant reference. Public web research and authorized browser observation are sufficient; no connector is required.

Record creator/site | source URL | date | viewport | observed mechanism | evidence type | transfer rule | leave-out list. Explain fit to audience/task. Award recognition proves neither usability, accessibility nor conversion. Do not copy third-party marks, proprietary fonts or imagery.

Inspect live behavior or actual video before claiming motion. Static screenshots are static evidence; inferred timing/stack must be labeled. Capture relevant start/middle/end and reduced-motion states when available/material. Translate the selected mechanism through client identity/components. Research does not authorize building projects or mass downloading assets.
